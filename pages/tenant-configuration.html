2. Tenant Configuration

UAE-specific tenant settings, features to disable for data sovereignty, and admin role delegation.

⚠ Critical: UAE Data Sovereignty

Abu Dhabi government entities are not permitted to process data outside the UAE. Several Fabric features route data through Azure OpenAI or third-party services that may process data outside your capacity's geographic region. These must be disabled at the tenant level before any data is loaded.

Standard Fabric workloads are NOT affected — Lakehouse, Warehouse, Notebooks, Data Factory pipelines, Semantic Models, OneLake, and Power BI reports all process data within your capacity's region.

Features to Disable (Cross-Region Processing)

The following features have been identified as potentially processing or storing data outside the UAE region. All must be disabled from the Fabric Admin Portal.

ℹ How to Disable
  1. Sign in to app.fabric.microsoft.com
  2. Click Settings (⚙) → Admin portal
  3. Navigate to Tenant settings
  4. Search for the exact setting name shown in the Tenant Setting Name column below
  5. Toggle to Disabled

Admin Roles & Delegation

Fabric admin roles should mirror your organisation's data ownership model and governance structure. Each role maps to a layer of accountability — from enterprise-wide policy at the top, down to domain-specific stewardship and project-level delivery. Aligning Fabric roles to existing organisational responsibilities ensures clear ownership, faster decision-making, and consistent enforcement of data governance policies across departments.

Role Scope Responsibilities Typical Government Persona
Fabric Administrator Entire tenant Sets organisation-wide policies (features to disable, security baseline). Tenant settings, feature toggles, capacity management, audit logs, user management. IT Director / Head of Digital Infrastructure / Platform Team Lead
Capacity Administrator Specific capacity (F-SKU) Delegated to the team managing Azure subscriptions and billing. Manage capacity settings, assign workspaces to capacity, monitor consumption, scale. Platform Engineer / Cloud Operations Lead
Domain Administrator Business domain (e.g., Tourism, Culture) Closer to the business — understands data sensitivity within their department. Override delegated tenant settings for the domain, manage domain-level governance. Department Data Lead / Chief Data Officer (department-level)
Workspace Administrator Individual workspace Team leads who manage day-to-day access for their project members. Manage workspace access, assign roles, configure Spark environments, connect to Git. Project Lead / Senior Data Engineer / Analytics Team Lead
⚡ Principle of Least Privilege
Not every IT staff member needs Fabric Admin rights. Delegate capacity and workspace admin roles to the appropriate team leads. Reserve Fabric Admin for the central platform team (1–3 people maximum).
